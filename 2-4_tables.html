Basic Accounting Tables

How to account a transaction:

  1. Identify changes in assets or liabilities + owner's equity.
  2. Mark changes in those columns for the transaction no.
  3. Ensure that assets equal liabilities + owner's equity, by either decreasing and increasing two amounts on the same side, or increasing/decreaing by the same amount on opposite sides.

Example:

  1. Owner invests $3,000.00 in business in the form of cash.
  2. Paid $300.00 for supplies.
  3. Bought $500.00 of supplies on account from J & M Suppliers.
  4. Collected $250.00 from sales in the form of cash.
